Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Short version: this website stores only a few small settings on your device — your language, and which of our own notices you have already seen. No advertising cookies, no analytics cookies, no tracking of any kind, nothing from third parties. That is why you see a small notice instead of an accept/reject banner.

1. What this policy covers

"Cookies" below means cookies and any similar browser storage (such as localStorage), in line with Spanish and EU law. This policy covers only this website (ghoosted.net). The Ghoosted browser Extension does not use cookies at all — it uses its own separate storage, chrome.storage.local, which is covered by our Privacy Policy, not by this page.

That is the entire list. ghosted_m only ever exists if you scanned the pairing QR from the extension — a feature you explicitly request. Nothing here identifies you, profiles you, or is readable by anyone else.

3. Why we don't ask for your consent for these

Spanish law (Article 22.2 LSSI-CE) and the EU ePrivacy Directive (Article 5(3)) exempt storage that is strictly necessary for a service you explicitly asked for, or used solely to remember a choice you made for the duration of your visits. The Article 29 Working Party's guidance on this exemption (Opinion 04/2012) specifically lists "user interface customisation" cookies — like a language preference or remembering a dismissed notice — as not requiring consent, and the AEPD's cookie guidance says a consent request must not be shown when only exempt storage is used. Every item in the table above is that kind of storage. That is why our notice simply informs you rather than asking you to accept or reject anything — there is nothing optional to accept.

4. What we do not use

No analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics), no advertising or retargeting pixels (e.g. Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel), no session-replay or heatmap tools, no fingerprinting, and no third-party embeds that set their own cookies. Fonts and flag icons are self-hosted, so loading this site makes zero requests to third-party servers. If any of this ever changes, we will update this page first and ask for your consent with a real accept/reject choice before any non-essential cookie is set — not just a notice.

5. Payments

When you buy Ghoosted Pro or Plus, checkout happens on Stripe's own domain. Any cookies Stripe sets during that process are Stripe's, governed by Stripe's Cookie Policy, not by us — we never see them.

6. How to control or delete them

You can delete any of these items at any time from your browser's site-data settings (for example, "Clear browsing data" → "Cookies and site data" for ghoosted.net). Doing so simply means the site re-detects your language, shows the notices again, or — for ghosted_m — unpairs the phone mirror until you scan the QR again. It has no effect on any purchase or licence key.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything here changes, and reflect that in the "Last updated" date. For our full identity and contact details as data controller, see the Privacy Policy and the Legal Notice, or write to hello@ghoosted.net.

3. Почему мы не спрашиваем твоего согласия на это

Испанское законодательство (ст. 22.2 LSSI-CE) и Директива ЕС о конфиденциальности в электронных коммуникациях (ст. 5(3)) освобождают от требования согласия хранение, которое строго необходимо для явно запрошенной тобой услуги, или используется исключительно для запоминания твоего выбора. Руководство Рабочей группы по статье 29 (Заключение 04/2012) прямо относит cookie для «настройки пользовательского интерфейса» — например, языковые предпочтения или запоминание закрытого уведомления — к не требующим согласия, а руководство AEPD по cookie уточняет, что запрос согласия не должен показываться, когда используется только освобождённое хранение. Каждый пункт таблицы выше — именно такое хранение. Поэтому наше уведомление просто информирует тебя, а не просит что-либо принять или отклонить — принимать здесь просто нечего.
